# Paid Time Off Policy

## POL-PTO-001 - Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 1.67 days of paid time off per completed month of
service, giving 20 days per calendar year. Employees with more than four years
of continuous service accrue at 2.08 days per month, giving 25 days per year.
Part-time employees accrue on a pro-rata basis according to contracted hours.
Accrual begins on the first day of employment, and time off may be taken before
it has fully accrued with manager approval, up to a maximum of five days in
advance.

## POL-PTO-002 - Requesting Time Off

Requests are submitted through the leave system and require manager approval
before travel is booked. Requests of five consecutive days or fewer should be
submitted at least two weeks in advance. Requests longer than five consecutive
days require four weeks notice. Managers may decline a request where team
coverage cannot be maintained, and must give a reason in writing within three
business days. Where two employees request the same period and only one can be
released, priority is given to the earlier submission.

## POL-PTO-003 - Carryover and Payout

Employees may carry a maximum of 5 unused days into the following calendar year.
Carried days must be used by 31 March, after which they lapse without payment.
Unused days beyond the carryover limit are forfeited at year end and are not
paid out, except where local law requires otherwise. On termination of
employment, accrued but unused days are paid at the employee's base daily rate,
and any days taken in advance of accrual are deducted from final pay where local
law permits. Employees serving notice may be directed to take accrued leave
during the notice period.

## POL-PTO-004 - Sick Leave

Sick leave is tracked separately from paid time off and does not reduce the
annual entitlement. Employees receive up to 10 paid sick days per calendar year.
Absences of three consecutive days or more require a medical certificate
submitted to People Operations. Sick days do not accrue and do not carry over.
Where an employee falls ill during a period of booked paid time off, those days
may be reclassified as sick leave on production of a medical certificate.
Chronic conditions requiring recurring absence are handled through the workplace
accommodation process rather than this section, and employees should contact
People Operations directly to begin that process. Managers are not permitted to
request diagnostic details and should limit enquiries to expected duration and
any adjustments required on return.

## POL-PTO-005 - Parental Leave

Employees with twelve months of continuous service are entitled to 16 weeks of
paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child, available to all
parents regardless of gender. Leave may be taken in up to three separate blocks
within the first eighteen months, provided each block is at least two weeks.
Paid time off continues to accrue during parental leave. Employees must give
eight weeks notice of their intended start date where circumstances allow.
Statutory entitlements in the employee's jurisdiction apply where they exceed
the provisions of this section.

## POL-PTO-006 - Unpaid Leave of Absence

An unpaid leave of absence of up to three months may be granted at the
discretion of the employee's manager and People Operations, normally for study,
extended family care, or personal circumstances. Paid time off does not accrue
during unpaid leave, and benefits continuation depends on the employee's
jurisdiction. Requests require eight weeks notice and a proposed return date.
Approval is not guaranteed and is assessed against team coverage and the
duration requested.
