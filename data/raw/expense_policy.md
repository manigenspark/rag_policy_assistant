# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## POL-EXP-001 - Purpose and Scope

This policy governs the reimbursement of business expenses incurred by employees
on behalf of the company. It applies to all employees worldwide, including those
on hybrid and fully remote arrangements. Expenses must be reasonable, necessary,
and directly connected to company business. Where a local statutory requirement
conflicts with this policy, the local requirement prevails and Finance
Operations will record the exception.

## POL-EXP-002 - Submission and Deadlines

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the date the expense
was incurred. Claims submitted after this window require written justification
from the employee's manager and are approved at the discretion of Finance
Operations. Claims older than 90 calendar days will not be reimbursed under any
circumstances. Reimbursements are paid through the standard payroll cycle
following approval, and employees should expect payment within two pay periods.

## POL-EXP-003 - Receipt Requirements

An itemised receipt is required for every expense of 25 USD or more. Card
statements are not accepted as substitutes for itemised receipts, because they
do not evidence what was purchased. For expenses below 25 USD, a written
description of the expense and its business purpose is sufficient. Receipts must
be legible and must show the vendor, date, amount, and currency. Where a receipt
has been lost, employees may submit a missing receipt declaration, limited to
three occurrences per calendar year.

## POL-EXP-004 - Pre-Approval Thresholds

Any single expense exceeding 500 USD requires written manager approval before
the expense is incurred. Approval must be obtained in advance through the
expense system and cannot be granted retrospectively except where the expense
arose from a genuine emergency affecting employee safety. Bookings that exceed
this threshold and were made without prior approval may be rejected in full, and
the cost becomes the personal responsibility of the employee. Recurring
subscriptions of any value require pre-approval regardless of the individual
charge amount, because the aggregate annual cost is the relevant figure.
Departmental budget holders may set lower thresholds for their own teams but may
not set higher ones.

## POL-EXP-005 - Meals and Entertainment

Meals taken alone while travelling on company business are reimbursed at actual
cost up to the applicable per diem. Meals involving external parties are treated
as business entertainment and require the names and employers of all attendees
to be recorded on the claim. Alcohol is reimbursable only where it forms part of
a client entertainment meal and is limited to two units per attendee. Team
social events require prior approval from a departmental budget holder and are
capped at 60 USD per attendee per event.

## POL-EXP-006 - International Travel and Per Diem

Employees travelling internationally on company business receive a per diem of
95 USD per day to cover meals and incidental expenses. The per diem is paid for
each full day of travel and is pro-rated at 50 percent for departure and return
days. Receipts are not required against the per diem, as it replaces itemised
meal claims for the duration of the trip. Lodging is reimbursed separately at
actual cost, capped at 280 USD per night in tier-one cities and 190 USD per
night elsewhere. Flights must be booked in economy class for journeys under six
hours. Currency conversion is calculated using the published rate on the
transaction date, and employees should not apply their own card provider's rate.

## POL-EXP-007 - Non-Reimbursable Items

The following are never reimbursable: traffic fines and parking penalties,
personal travel insurance, in-flight entertainment and internet upgrades,
airport lounge memberships purchased individually, childcare, pet care, personal
grooming, and losses arising from personal negligence. Expenses incurred while
working from another country under a personal remote work arrangement are also
non-reimbursable, as set out in POL-RMT-006.
