# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## POL-EXP-001 - Purpose and Scope

This policy governs the reimbursement of business expenses incurred by employees
on behalf of the company. It applies to all permanent employees. Expenses must
be reasonable, necessary, and directly connected to company business. Where a
local statutory requirement conflicts with this policy, the local requirement
prevails and Finance Operations will record the exception.

## POL-EXP-002 - Submission and Deadlines

Expense claims must be submitted within 60 calendar days of the date the expense
was incurred. Claims submitted after this window require written justification
from the employee's manager and are approved at the discretion of Finance
Operations. Claims older than 120 calendar days will not be reimbursed under any
circumstances. Reimbursements are paid by separate bank transfer twice monthly
following approval, and employees should allow up to three weeks from approval
for funds to clear. Claims spanning more than one calendar month should be
submitted separately so that each month can be allocated to the correct
accounting period.

## POL-EXP-003 - Receipt Requirements

An itemised receipt is required for every expense of 75 USD or more. Card
statements may be accepted as supporting evidence where an itemised receipt is
not available. For expenses below 75 USD, a written description of the expense
and its business purpose is sufficient. Receipts must be legible and must show
the vendor, date, amount, and currency. Handwritten receipts are accepted where
a vendor does not issue printed documentation, provided the vendor name and
address are shown. Foreign language receipts should be accompanied by a short
English summary of the items purchased.

## POL-EXP-004 - Pre-Approval Thresholds

Any single expense exceeding 1,000 USD requires written manager approval before
the expense is incurred. Approval should normally be obtained in advance, but
may be granted retrospectively where the employee provides a business
justification within five working days. Recurring subscriptions above 1,000 USD
annually require pre-approval. Departmental budget holders may set their own
thresholds for their teams.

## POL-EXP-005 - Meals and Entertainment

Meals taken alone while travelling on company business are reimbursed at actual
cost up to the applicable per diem. Meals involving external parties are treated
as business entertainment and require the names and employers of all attendees
to be recorded on the claim. Alcohol is reimbursable where it forms part of a
client entertainment meal. Team social events require prior approval from a
departmental budget holder and are capped at 40 USD per attendee per event.

## POL-EXP-006 - International Travel and Per Diem

Employees travelling internationally on company business receive a per diem of
75 USD per day to cover meals and incidental expenses. The per diem is paid for
each full day of travel and is pro-rated at 50 percent for departure and return
days. Receipts are not required against the per diem, as it replaces itemised
meal claims for the duration of the trip. Lodging is reimbursed separately at
actual cost, capped at 210 USD per night in tier-one cities and 150 USD per
night elsewhere. Flights must be booked in economy class for journeys under
eight hours.

## POL-EXP-007 - Non-Reimbursable Items

The following are never reimbursable: traffic fines and parking penalties,
personal travel insurance, in-flight entertainment and internet upgrades,
airport lounge memberships purchased individually, childcare, pet care, and
personal grooming. Upgrades to premium economy or business class are not
reimbursable unless a medical need has been documented in advance with People
Operations. Losses arising from personal negligence, including unattended
baggage and damage to hotel property, remain the responsibility of the
employee.
