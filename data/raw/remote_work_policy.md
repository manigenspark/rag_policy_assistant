# Remote Work Policy

## POL-RMT-001 - Scope and Eligibility

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees, excluding
contractors engaged through third-party agencies. Eligibility for remote work
requires that an employee has completed their initial ninety-day onboarding
period and holds a role that does not require on-site presence, such as
laboratory work or facilities management. Managers approve remote arrangements
at the team level and may revoke an arrangement with four weeks written notice
where business needs change. Employees whose roles become ineligible following
a transfer will be given the same notice period.

## POL-RMT-002 - Core Collaboration Hours

All remote employees must be reachable between 10:00 and 16:00 in the time zone
of their assigned office. Meetings requiring cross-team attendance are scheduled
exclusively within this window. Employees working outside their assigned office
time zone by more than three hours must agree a written overlap schedule with
their manager. Calendar availability must be kept current, and extended absences
during core hours require advance notice.

## POL-RMT-003 - Home Office Stipend

Remote employees may claim a one-time home office stipend of 800 USD within
their first six months of remote status, and a recurring annual allowance of
300 USD thereafter for consumables and replacements. Eligible items include
desks, chairs, monitors, keyboards, and lighting. The stipend does not cover
rent, utilities, or internet service, which are addressed separately under the
connectivity allowance. All stipend claims are processed as ordinary expense
reimbursements and are therefore subject to the submission deadlines and receipt
requirements set out in the Expense Reimbursement Policy, in particular
POL-EXP-002 and POL-EXP-003. Claims submitted without an itemised receipt will
be returned unpaid.

## POL-RMT-004 - Equipment and Security

The company issues a laptop and, on request, one external monitor to every
remote employee. Company-issued devices must run the managed endpoint agent and
receive operating system updates within fourteen days of release. Employees may
not store customer data on personal devices or in personal cloud accounts under
any circumstances. Home networks must use WPA2 or stronger encryption, and
default router credentials must be changed. Where an employee works from a
shared or co-working space, screen privacy filters are mandatory and voice calls
covering confidential matters must be taken in an enclosed room. Lost or stolen
equipment must be reported to the security team within twenty-four hours of
discovery, after which the device will be remotely wiped. Employees leaving the
company must return all hardware within five business days of their final
working day, using the prepaid shipping label provided by IT operations. Failure
to return equipment may result in deduction from final pay where local law
permits. Personal peripherals such as keyboards, mice, and docking stations may
be connected to company devices provided they do not require the installation of
unsigned drivers. Employees must not connect company laptops to public charging
ports or untrusted USB devices, and should use a mains adapter when charging in
airports, hotels, and other public spaces. Printing of documents classified as
confidential or above is prohibited outside company premises. Requests for
additional monitors, adjustable desks, or ergonomic accessories beyond the
standard issue are handled as workplace accommodation requests through People
Operations rather than as equipment requests, and may require supporting
documentation from a qualified practitioner before approval is granted.

## POL-RMT-005 - Hybrid Attendance Requirements

Employees assigned to a hybrid arrangement attend their designated office a
minimum of eight days per calendar month. Attendance days are recorded through
building access badges. Teams may cluster their attendance days to maximise
overlap, provided the monthly minimum is met. Travel days to another company
office count toward the minimum. Employees on approved medical accommodation are
exempt.

## POL-RMT-006 - Working From Another Country

Employees may work from a country other than their country of employment for up
to thirty calendar days per rolling twelve-month period, subject to written
approval from both their manager and the tax compliance team. Requests must be
submitted at least twenty-one days before departure. Approval will be withheld
where the destination creates a permanent establishment risk or where local
immigration rules prohibit remote work on a visitor visa. Any travel costs
incurred during such arrangements are personal and are not reimbursable, and
employees should not confuse this provision with company-directed international
business travel, which is governed by POL-EXP-006. Extensions beyond thirty days
require an international assignment, which is handled as a separate process by
Global Mobility.
